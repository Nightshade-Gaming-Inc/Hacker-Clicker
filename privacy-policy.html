Hacker Clicker Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Hacker Clicker: Idle Tycoon is a single-player game made by Range Ban. This policy explains what happens to information about you when you play. The short version: we don't collect anything ourselves. The ads are served by Google AdMob, which does collect some data, as described below.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. We never receive your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers or your save.

Your save data

Your progress, settings and achievements are stored only on your device, along with an automatic backup copy that is used if the main save is ever damaged. If you use the in-game backup code feature, the code is yours to copy and keep; it is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your save and its backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: ads between play sessions at natural breaks, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a bonus. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

  • Device and account identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and ad performance

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, is encrypted in transit, and is handled under Google's privacy policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Buying Remove Ads, the Cyber Maid or the Lawyer turns off the ads that play between sessions. Rewarded ads stay optional.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, Cyber Maid and Lawyer on Retainer) are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. When the game opens, it asks Google Play which items you own, so purchases are restored on a new phone and refunded purchases are removed. That check happens on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Checking the time

To stop changed phone clocks from faking idle earnings and daily rewards, the game asks a public server (Google, Apple or Cloudflare) for the current time when it opens. This is a plain HTTPS request with no information about you. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to that server, under that company's own privacy policy. We receive nothing.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game can use it to keep a cloud copy of your save, unlock achievements and post your scores to leaderboards. That data (your save, achievements, scores and your Play Games profile name) is stored by Google under Google's privacy policy so your progress can follow you to a new device. We never receive it. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, when your idle earnings are full or your daily drop is ready) directly on your device. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

Sharing and rating

The Share button opens your phone's own share menu with a link to the game; we don't see whether you share or who with. The Rate button opens the game's Google Play page.

Deleting your data

Because we don't hold any data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. Data collected by Google for ads is controlled through your Android ad settings and your Google account privacy settings.

Children

The game is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
