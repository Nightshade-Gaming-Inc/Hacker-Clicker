Hacker Clicker Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Hacker Clicker is a single-player game made by Range Ban. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: we don't collect anything ourselves; the ads in the free version are served by Google, which does.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. We never see your name, email, contacts, location or device identifiers.

Your save data

Your progress, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. If you use the in-game backup feature, the code it shows you is yours to copy and keep; it is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your save.

Checking the time

To stop changed phone clocks from faking idle earnings and daily rewards, the game asks a public server (Google, Apple or Cloudflare) for the current time when it opens. This is a plain HTTPS request with no information about you. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to that server, under that company's own privacy policy. We receive nothing.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it to keep a cloud copy of your save, unlock achievements and post your scores to leaderboards. That data (your save, achievements, scores and your Play Games profile name) is stored by Google under Google's privacy policy so your progress can follow you to a new device. We never receive it. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, when your idle earnings are full or your daily drop is ready) directly on your device. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

Purchases

The optional in-app purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details; the game only learns whether the purchase was made so it can unlock it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, including an optional rewarded ad you choose to watch. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, under Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings. Buying the Lawyer removes ads for good.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
